# Prompte për fotot me AI (Kanagjegji i Ernës)

Përdori në Midjourney, DALL·E / ChatGPT, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo ose Ideogram.
Kërko format vertikal (3:4), pastaj ruaji fotot në dosjen `images/` me emrat `foto1.jpg` … `foto6.jpg`.

Këshillë: nëse do që fotot t'i ngjajnë Ernës, ngarko një foto të saj në mjet (me lejen e saj) dhe kërko "same woman". Përndryshe përdor personazhe të krijuar nga AI.

## Prompti bazë (shtoje në fund të secilit)
`warm golden-hour light, photorealistic, 85mm portrait, rich red and black and gold palette, authentic Albanian folk culture, respectful, high detail, 3:4 vertical`

## Foto 1 — Erna me mindil
`Young Albanian woman in traditional Kosovo folk costume, embroidered red and black dress with gold thread, silver filigree jewelry, a red embroidered mindil (handkerchief) held gracefully in her hand, stone courtyard with a traditional carpet (qilim) behind her,` + prompti bazë

## Foto 2 — Erna me stoli
`Portrait of a young Albanian woman for a henna night (nata e kënës), decorated hands with henna, silver necklaces, embroidered velvet vest, soft candlelight, red fabrics in the background,` + prompti bazë

## Foto 3 — Vallja e mindilit
`Group of Albanian dancers in a circle folk dance (valle), lead dancer waving a red and white mindil high in the air, traditional embroidered costumes, motion blur on the cloth, village square at sunset,` + prompti bazë

## Foto 4 — Me familjen
`Young Albanian woman in the center with her mother and sisters, all in matching traditional costumes and headscarves, holding mindil together, warm smiles, ornate qilim hanging behind,` + prompti bazë

## Foto 5 — Detaje
`Close-up still life of Albanian traditional henna-night details: red embroidered mindil, silver filigree jewelry, henna in a small dish, woven red and black textile, on a wooden table, shallow depth of field,` + prompti bazë

## Foto 6 — Gratë në valle
`Albanian women in traditional folk dresses and headscarves dancing together, each holding a colorful mindil, smiling, joyful, courtyard decorated with red cloths and candles,` + prompti bazë
